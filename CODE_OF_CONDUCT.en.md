# Code of Conduct

Respect participants with different backgrounds and levels of experience in discussions, Issues and contributions.
Discuss problems through identifiable behavior and evidence, and allow reasoned disagreement.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, disclosure of another person's private information,
and continued unwanted contact are prohibited. Discussions and examples must not expose customer data or credentials.

The maintainer may request changes, remove inappropriate content, restrict participation or exclude repeated offenders,
considering the facts, severity and recurrence.

Report violations privately through an available contact channel on the maintainer's
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/Sunnyshinnny776). If no private channel is available, submit a contact request
without private details. Do not disclose sensitive information about the people involved in a public Issue.
The maintainer uses report information only to the extent needed to handle the report.
